The Damage Formula

When a creature attacks another, damage scales upward with the attacker's Attack and downward with the defender's Defense. The hero's own Attack and Defense are added on top of the creature's own values before the calculation runs.

// Basic damage modifier — two equivalent forms: modifier = (1 + 5% × ATK) / (1 + 5% × DEF) modifier = (20 + ATK) / (20 + DEF) // Damage delta (how much the modifier shifts base damage): delta = (1 + 5% × ATK) / (1 + 5% × DEF) − 1

Example from the source: a Swordsman (ATK 4) striking a Skeleton (DEF 1) gets a modifier of (20+4)/(20+1) = 1.14. Swordsman base damage 2–3 becomes 2.28–3.42, rounded by standard rules (≥.5 up, <.5 down).

Total Damage — All Factors

UnitCount × UnitDamage × A/D-Modifier × AllTypeMods × TagBasedMods × OtherIndependent × Luck
  • All-Type Damage Modifiers — affect every damage source. e.g. Radiant Armor, Naira's Way, Adaptation (Scorpion ability).
  • Tag-Based Damage — affect specific damage types only. e.g. Offense / Defense skills, Blessing, Favorable Wind, Optical Illusion. Rule of thumb: if the effect names a damage type, it's tag-based; if not, it's all-type.
  • Other Independent — range penalty, wall reduction, anti-summon damage, Apex Predator (Reaver), Jousting Bonus (Cavalry), Luck.
  • Attack / Defense modifier has no upper limit, lower limit of 0.
  • All-Type damage reduction has no upper limit.
  • Tag-based bonuses/reductions are added within a category, then multiplied across categories. Minimum result is 10% of incoming damage.
  • Tag-based modifiers for attackers and defenders are calculated separately.
  • Final damage is always at least 1 point, regardless of adjustments.
  • This simulator implements the Basic Modifier only — i.e. the Attack/Defense interaction at the core of every damage calculation. Skill bonuses and tag effects are out of scope.

Worked Example — Tag-Based Cap

100 Archangels strike Animated Armor. Initial A/D modifier: (36+20)/(12+20) = 1.75. Archangel damage 50–75.

(100) × (50–75) × (1.75) × (0.6) × (0.1) = 525 – 787.5 → 788 Step 1: 100 × (50–75) = 5,000 – 7,500 (raw stack damage) Step 2: × 1.75 (A/D modifier) = 8,750 – 13,125 Step 3: × 0.6 (Radiant Armor) = 5,250 – 7,875 (all-type reduction) Step 4: × 0.1 (capped tag-based) = 525 – 787.5 round up → 788 final damage

How To Use

A quick guide to each piece of the interface
Faction & Unit Selectors
Faction — click to open a faction picker and switch between all 6 factions; the unit list resets to that faction's first unit automatically. Unit opens a dropdown listing every creature grouped by tier, with stats for quick comparison. Choosing a unit instantly applies its HP, Attack, Defense, and Damage values to that side.
Stack
How many creatures are in the stack (0–9999). Each value you type becomes that unit's new baseline — the value Reset Sequence will restore the stack to. Changing it also refills the top creature to full HP.
Stat Cards (HP, Attack, Defense, Damage)
Each stat shows an editable number you can type directly into. Click anywhere on the card to open a slider underneath — drag to adjust visually. HP ranges 0–1000, Attack & Defense 0–200. Damage is a range (min & max) because the formula needs it.
HP Bar & Number
Shown above each unit's body. Tracks the current HP of just the topmost creature in the stack — the one taking the next hit. When it hits 0, that creature dies and the next one steps up at full HP. Modifying the HP stat refills the bar to the new max.
Strike Buttons
Triggers a single attack from that side immediately. Use when you want to manually control who strikes when.
Battle Sequence
Click the ◀ or ▶ arrow to pick which side strikes first — that arrow lights up gold. Then click the center "Battle Sequence" to perform one alternating strike per click. The active side's arrow shows whose turn is next.
Reset Sequence
Clears the first-attacker selection and restores both units to their baseline: stack returns to the last value you typed in, HP refills to max. Battle log is preserved — clear it separately with the Clear button on the log.
Battle Log
Shows every strike: attacker, damage rolled, kills, and the formula breakdown so you can see exactly how the number was reached.
Table & Information Tab
A clean reference for the damage formulas, the full Attack/Defense delta table, common modifier patterns, and the special rules (tag-based cap, all-type uncapped, min-1-damage, etc.).
